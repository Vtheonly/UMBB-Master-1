> them, never as a standalone learning path of their own.

The in-depth theory behind the image-processing library notes lives in the
`Image Processing/` chapters; theory explains *why*, the library note explains
*how to call it*. Notes 25 and 26 are the exhaustive engineering references
for the two flagship edge-preserving operators — read them alongside
[[02.6. Structural Edge-Preserving Smoothing (Nagao 9-Sector Multi-Directional Filter)]]
and [[02.7. Non-Linear Boundary-Preserving Filtering (Bilateral Joint Spatial-Radiometric Filter)]],
and before the laboratory [[09.5. TP2 Part 2 - Edge-Preserving Non-Linear Smoothing with Nagao Filter]].
